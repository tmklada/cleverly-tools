## What Is a WiFi QR Code?

A WiFi QR code is a standard QR code that encodes your network's SSID (network name), password, and security type. When a smartphone camera scans it, the phone automatically connects to that WiFi network — no manual entry needed.

Modern iPhones (iOS 11+) and Android phones (Android 9+) support WiFi QR codes natively through the default camera app.

## Why Use a WiFi QR Code?

- **Guests connect instantly** — perfect for Airbnb, cafés, offices, and events
- **No more misread passwords** — eliminates typos with special characters
- **Professional appearance** — print it on a card, frame it, or add it to a welcome packet
- **Works offline** — the QR code is generated in your browser; nothing is sent to a server

## Tips for a Better WiFi QR Code

- **Test before printing:** Scan the QR code yourself before distributing to confirm it works
- **Use high resolution:** Download the largest image size available for clear printing
- **Update when password changes:** Generate a new QR code any time you change the WiFi password
- **Add a label:** Write "Scan to Connect" above or below the QR code so guests know what it does

## Is It Safe to Share a WiFi QR Code?

Yes — with some caveats. The QR code contains your password in encoded form. Anyone who scans it gets access to your network, so treat it like your printed password. Do not post it publicly online.

For guest access, consider creating a separate guest network with its own password, then generating a QR code for that network only. This keeps your main devices isolated.
